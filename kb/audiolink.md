## What AudioLink is

AudioLink is remote front-of-house (FOH) and broadcast audio for churches. A church that needs a consistent mix, in the room, on the stream, or both, gets that mix covered instead of hoping the right volunteer is available every Sunday.

Yes, AudioLink does remote mixing. The team can share how the setup works live on the call. Avery does not invent the signal path.

The public booking page describes the path after a church says yes:

1. A 30-minute call. Stacked, no buffer. The team learns the room and whether AudioLink fits.
2. A custom proposal. The church reviews it and can sign digitally. Onboarding starts after they sign.
3. The rig is configured, tested, and shipped to the church.
4. The church's team installs it while AudioLink guides them live on Slack. Most installs take under an hour.
5. Go live. The goal is a Sunday that sounds settled in the room, online, or both.
